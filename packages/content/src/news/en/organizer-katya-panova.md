Our team is growing! Meet Katya Panova, now one of the MoscowCSS organizers, and with her the meetup gets a design track of its own.

- Design lead at SberInvestments
- Academic director of the ITMO master's programme in design and development of digital products
- PhD student in philosophy at Bauman University
- Yacht captain licensed to sail a yacht up to 24 metres or skipper a motor yacht up to 16 metres in international waters\
  (Russia did not grant it)

Why now? AI is changing how we work, and frontend developers and designers are especially good together right now: the programme gets wider, and both sides make each other stronger. We already have design talks regularly, but we ran them mostly by instinct. Now we are going to do it well!

Katya takes over the whole design track:

- reviews design proposals: the topic, the level, and whether it is an ad;
- works with speakers at rehearsals: structure, delivery, slides;
- invites strong people from the design community, where we have few ways in so far.

If you are a designer and have long wanted to give a talk, now is the time. Please give her a warm welcome!
