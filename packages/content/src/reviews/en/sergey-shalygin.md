I love MoscowCSS, where I gave my first talk. What pleased me most was how warmly they helped me prepare: the team supported me at every step, gave advice and calmed me down before I went on stage.

I had often been there as a guest, and I especially like the atmosphere: not only seasoned experts come, but also people just starting out. Everyone is responsive and pleasant, you can talk to anyone and share something new. We swapped contacts, joked during the breaks and, of course, had a tasty meal.

I left feeling that I had found new friends and colleagues. I will definitely come back!
