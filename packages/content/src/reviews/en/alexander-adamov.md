MoscowCSS is a community of people who help each other keep up with what is happening in the world of development.

Here you can hear talks on current topics, from new APIs to trends like AI (where would we be without it). But MoscowCSS is not only about technology. People share real cases, problems and experience that help you look at the industry from different angles, learn something new and refresh the basics.

It is also a chance to look behind the scenes of different companies, find out what challenges developers face, swap experience, make useful connections and simply find friends.

Special thanks to Roma and the whole committee for their optimism in such a tough time for tech! I'm inspired by how the team keeps finding new ideas for projects and looks at our profession from different angles.
