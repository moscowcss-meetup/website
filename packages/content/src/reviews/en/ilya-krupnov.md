MoscowCSS gave me my first chance to speak in front of an audience I did not know: before that I had only given talks inside my company. I got a lot of positive emotions, a lively response and applause at the end of the talk, even though I was very nervous before going on stage.

For me the MoscowCSS community is cosy, warm and at the same time truly professional. For anyone who wants to try being a speaker, or just to hear interesting talks on current web development topics, MoscowCSS is a meetup you have to attend.

Special thanks to the organizers: their support while I was preparing and during the talk itself was incredibly valuable.
