Thanks to Roma Troitsky, we got to know the MoscowCSS team and held a joint meetup at Sber. The team helped put together an excellent programme, find speakers and attract a large audience both offline and online: I recommend booking bigger halls for conferences like this 🙂

I'd especially like to mention the quality of the MoscowCSS audience. These are developers with different experience and levels of expertise, who genuinely care about professional conversation and sharing knowledge. Gathering so many people around you and earning their trust is perhaps the best assessment of the organisers' work!

It was easy and pleasant to work with the team, and I would gladly repeat the experience!
