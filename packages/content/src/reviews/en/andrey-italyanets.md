For me, meetups have always been something more than a chance to hear interesting talks. They mean new acquaintances, sharing experience and a chance to look at familiar things from a slightly different angle. Sometimes you come to hear one topic and leave with completely different thoughts and ideas. That is probably the most valuable part, but for me MoscowCSS is also about people. About those who are truly passionate about what they do, who do all this not to tick a box, but because they find it interesting themselves and want to share it with others.

I especially want to mention Roma Troitsky. Some people have so much energy, genuine interest in what they do and some incredible positivity that it rubs off on everyone around them. Roma is one of them. And I think it is largely thanks to him and the people who organise all this that MoscowCSS has such an atmosphere. When a person puts into something not only their time and effort but also a piece of themselves, you can always feel it. And at some point you realise that you come not only for knowledge, but for the people, the conversations and that very atmosphere which a good programme of talks alone cannot create.

Thank you, Roma, and the whole MoscowCSS team for what you do. For your enthusiasm, your openness and for sharing with us not only knowledge but a piece of your inner world. That truly means a lot!

And thanks to the meetups I have met many great people who have become close to me over time. That is a joy of its own!
