I really love MoscowCSS; I try to visit every event. They’re awesome, and here’s why:

Firstly, the speakers do their best to prepare interesting and useful talks that cover a wide variety of topics. It’s never boring here!

Secondly, the organisers work hard to ensure the meetups run smoothly. If something goes wrong, they quickly resolve the issue. That’s exactly how it should be!

Thirdly, the atmosphere. MoscowCSS is all about a relaxed vibe, meeting lovely people and having entertaining conversations. Every time, I realise that going to the meetup was absolutely worthy.

Many thanks to you all for everything!
