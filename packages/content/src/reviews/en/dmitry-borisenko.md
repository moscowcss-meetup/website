I used to come to Moscow CSS now and then as a listener. But after getting to know the organizers, Roman and Egor, I decided I wanted to give a talk too. My talk at Moscow CSS was my first public talk in front of a large audience.

At the rehearsal they helped me shape the talk so it fit into the time slot, and I went on stage much calmer. After the talk, people in the room and on the livestream asked a lot of interesting questions, and I had to answer them (: I love sharing knowledge, so I'll be back with a new talk.
