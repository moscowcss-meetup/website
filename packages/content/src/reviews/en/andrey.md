**I think Moscow CSS is the best community!**  
When I was only starting to "come out of the shadows", that is, out of my usual circle, I met a wonderful and charismatic speaker at events, Roman Troitsky, who invited me to my first Moscow CSS meetup.  
**A cosy crowd, networking, positive and energetic people**: I have not regretted becoming part of the community several years ago. I can't always dig deep into the specifics of CSS, but for finding connections, talking and discussing development, **the community is perfect.** It is in this community that I found myself and my team for building our product, Hello,flat!  
**I wish the community to flourish, to grow even bigger and to win the trust of IT communities not only in the city, but across the country and the world 🌏**
