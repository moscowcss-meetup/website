We take no donations: we are non-profit. People help with a talk, [hands for the site](https://github.com/moscowcss-meetup/website/issues), a venue or a joint meetup.\
What we need and the numbers for devrels are on the page [“How to help the meetup”](https://css.moscow/en/help/).
