title: "Hyperlinks in music: who stole from whom and why"
---

Does everything in music have to be new? And if we recognise something, is it always plagiarism? Do composers steal or borrow: from themselves, from colleagues, from folk tradition? And to what end?

Or maybe inserting someone else's music, or one's own, carries a new, extra meaning and, like a hyperlink, takes us elsewhere, like a time machine or a portal to other worlds?

How was other people's music used in the Reformation era, how did Bach turn his works into hypertext, how did Mozart advertise himself, how did Tchaikovsky turn a folk song into a confession of his soul, and how did Mozart write one of the most famous tangos of the 20th century back in the 18th?

We'll get to the bottom of all this in our investigation, with the help of an old gramophone.
