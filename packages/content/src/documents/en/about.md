Moscow CSS is a community of interface developers and designers who love CSS, browser technology and design. The community has been around since 2015. Since 2 March 2017 we have held more than thirty meetups: talks on native CSS features, design systems, animation, accessibility, typography and frontend tooling, and on how a developer grows and finds common ground with designers.

## How the meetups work

A meetup is an evening of several talks, questions to the speakers and networking afterwards. Entry is free, attending in person needs a registration, and many meetups can be watched online. Companies host the meetups in their offices: over the years we have been hosted by Yandex, Rambler, Tinkoff, Raiffeisenbank, Ozon, Cian, Kaspersky, MTS AI, Skillbox, Avito, red_mad_robot, MTS Fintech, School 21, Zvuk and others.

We are about development after all, so technical talks make up most of the programme, and a meetup usually has one talk on management or soft skills.

## Recordings and archive

The programmes of all meetups, the talks and the speakers are collected on this site: start with the [list of all meetups](https://css.moscow/en/events/) or the [search](https://css.moscow/en/search/). Recordings of the talks are on the [Moscow CSS YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Moscowcss) and on VK Video, linked from the meetup and talk pages.
