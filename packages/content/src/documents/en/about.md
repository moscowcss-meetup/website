Moscow CSS is a community of interface developers and designers who love CSS, browser technology and design. The community has been around since 2015. Since 2 March 2017 we have held more than thirty meetups: talks about native CSS features, design systems, animation, accessibility, typography and frontend tools, and also about how a developer grows and works with designers.

## How the meetups work

A meetup is an evening of several talks, questions to the speakers and networking afterwards. Entry is free, attending in person needs a registration, and many meetups can be watched online. Companies host the meetups in their offices: over the years we have been hosted by Yandex, Rambler, Tinkoff, Raiffeisenbank, Ozon, Cian, Kaspersky, MTS AI, Skillbox, Avito, red_mad_robot, MTS Fintech, School 21, Zvuk and others.

## Recordings and the archive

The programme of every meetup, its talks and speakers are on this site: start with the [list of all meetups](https://css.moscow/en/events/) or the [search](https://css.moscow/en/search/). Talk recordings are on the [Moscow CSS YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Moscowcss) and on VK Video; the links are on the meetup and talk pages.

## Contacts

Moscow CSS has no office and no phone: the community lives in Telegram, and meetups take place at the offices of partner companies. The address of each meetup is on its page.

### Announcements and news

The [Telegram channel @moscowcss](https://t.me/moscowcss) is the main channel of the community. It publishes meetup announcements, registration and stream links, and recordings and photos after each meetup.

### Discussions and questions

The [chat @moscowcss_chat](https://t.me/moscowcss_chat) is where you ask the speakers questions during a stream and talk about CSS between meetups.

### Speak at a meetup

Read the [speaker guide](https://gist.github.com/LenaVladi/5f196be72b5a2bca9a4c5869cf2b2088) and write to the organisers in Telegram: [@RinaPa](https://t.me/RinaPa), [@DreamShaded](https://t.me/DreamShaded), [@Ppppst](https://t.me/Ppppst). Tell them the topic and a few words about the talk: they will help with the programme and the preparation. Speakers' data is processed under the [personal data policy](https://css.moscow/en/privacy/).
