- Composer, member of the Union of Composers. Writes for various instruments, choir and orchestra, as well as music for plays and films
- Conductor, including the Grand Children's Choir and the Academic Orchestra of Radio and TV
- Graduate of the Gnessin Academy, the Moscow Conservatory and its assistantship-internship programme
- Teaches every subject of the music theory and composition course
- Lecturer and host of educational talks on classical and 20th-century Russian music (the "This Is Opera" club and the "Music Lovers' Club" at the ZIL Cultural Centre)
